# Employee Guide 1 — Getting Started

> [!NOTE]
> This guide is for **Employee users**. If you are an admin, see the [Admin Getting Started Guide](../admin/01-getting-started.md).

---

## Welcome

The FMP Timesheet Application is used to log your daily work hours, track projects, and manage leave. This guide explains how to log in, navigate the app, and understand what you can access.

---

## 1. How to Log In

### Step-by-Step

1. Open the application URL in your browser.
2. **Select your username** from the dropdown list.
   - Usernames look like: `john.doe`, `priya.sharma`
   - If your name is not in the list, contact your admin.
3. **Enter your password** in the password field.
4. **Solve the CAPTCHA** shown on screen.
   - It is a **6-character code** with letters and numbers (e.g., `Xk7P2m`).
   - Type it exactly as shown — it is **case-sensitive**.
   - If you can't read it, reload the page for a new CAPTCHA.
5. Click **Login**.

---

## 2. What Happens After Login

You are taken to the **Timesheet** page by default. The sidebar on the left shows the modules you have access to.

---

## 3. What Modules Can You Access?

Your access depends on your role and any extra permissions your admin has granted.

### Default Employee Access

| Module | Access Level |
|--------|-------------|
| 🗒️ Timesheet | ✅ Full access (your own entries only) |
| 📁 Projects | ✅ View only |
| 🔄 Project Updates | ✅ View only (unless extra permission granted) |

### With Extra Permissions

| Permission | Additional Access |
|------------|------------------|
| `project_update_access` | ✅ Edit Project Update grid |
| `allow_add_project` | ✅ Access Add Project staging area |

> [!NOTE]
> If you need access to a module that isn't visible in your sidebar, contact your admin and ask them to enable the relevant permission for your account.

---

## 4. Failed Login & Account Lockout

| Situation | What Happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| Wrong password (1–4 times) | Error message shown, try again |
| Wrong password **5 times** | Account locked for **15 minutes** |

> [!WARNING]
> After 5 failed attempts, you **cannot log in** for 15 minutes. If this happens by mistake, wait 15 minutes and try again, or ask your admin to reset your password.

---

## 5. What to Do If You Forget Your Password

You cannot reset your own password from the login screen.

**Steps:**
1. Contact your admin.
2. Ask them to reset your password from the Employee Management module.
3. Use the new temporary password to log in.
4. Immediately change it using the in-app password change feature (see [Profile & Security](./06-profile-and-security.md)).

---

## 6. Navigating the App

The sidebar is your main navigation:

- Click any module name to open it.
- The active module is highlighted.
- Your username is shown at the bottom of the sidebar.

---

## 7. The Weekly Timesheet Lock

The system may restrict timesheet submissions on certain days. This is controlled by your admin.

If you try to submit a timesheet entry on a blocked day, you will see a lockout message.

**What to do:**
- Wait until the lockout lifts.
- Contact your admin if you need to submit outside normal hours.

---

## 8. Logging Out / Ending Your Session

There is no explicit logout button. Your session is tied to your browser tab:
- Closing the browser tab ends your session.
- Refreshing the page keeps you logged in.
- If you share a computer, always close the browser tab completely after use.
